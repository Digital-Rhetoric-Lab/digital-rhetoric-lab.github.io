Module 2: Inquiry into Digital Network Issues

This module will prepare you for Module 3. It will help you identify a specific issue, understand why it matters, who is affected, use sources to learn more, and point toward a possible intervention or prototype.

Explore the Issue

In Module 1, you wrote about your own digital literacy moments. In this module, you’ll connect those experiences to bigger issues in the digital world. A personal experience can raise larger questions about audience, trust, algorithms, access, identity, privacy, activism, AI, or how information moves online.

As you explore these issues, you may focus on your own experience, a group/community you belong to, a field you’re interested in, or a broader population affected by digital networks.

Identifying Good Sources

Look for current, relevant evidence from qualified authors or organizations. Check the source’s purpose, supporting evidence, publication context, and whether other reliable sources confirm the information. You can also use the TRAAP test: timeframe, relevance, authority, accuracy, and purpose.
